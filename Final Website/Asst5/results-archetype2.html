Health Obsessed

The Health Obsessed archetype builds their life around wellness, always looking for the next thing that might make them feel a little clearer or more in control. Their feed fills up with vegan meals, yoga routines, meditation clips, and long threads about clean eating. What starts as wanting to feel good can turn into a kind of ritual, something they rely on to steady themselves when everything else feels overwhelming. They’re constantly fine-tuning their habits, convinced that if they get it all just right, they’ll finally feel balanced.
